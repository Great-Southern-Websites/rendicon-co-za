02 Equipment schedule

  • 01

    Cooker

    All raw products (feathers, DOAs and mortalities) are loaded into the cooker, which uses steam to hydrolyse and sterilise the raw product. This is dried to a 10% moisture content and delivers a 75% to 80% protein product.

    A uniquely designed super high-vap shaft inside the cooker is used to turn the product.

    We offer design and manufacturing of complete cookers of all sizes, including the cooker shell, super high-vap cooker shafts and drive units.

  • 02

    Rotary screens

    The final product from the cooker is placed onto the rotary screen to eliminate all metal, plastic or any foreign objects left in the product.

  • 03

    Stainless steel condensers and cooling towers

    Condenses and cools vapour steam from the cooker after the cooking process.

    The cooking temperature is brought down from 100°C to 20°C to eliminate odour and reduce environmental impact.

  • 04

    Stainless steel fans

    Extracts vapour from the condenser, which is channelled through to the bio-filter system.

  • 05

    Stainless steel conveyors and screw conveyors

    Transfers the discharged product from the cooker to the rotary screen and onto a bagging system, to reduce labour cost and increase time efficiency.

  • 06

    Stainless steel inline trap or stainless steel cyclones

    Prevents transfer from the cooker onto the condenser after the blowdown cycle has been done.

    This keeps the system clean and prevents blockages.

  • 07

    Dewatering screen

    The water content of the feathers has to be reduced by use of a dewatering screen, to reduce excessive amounts of water in the cooker, which in return results in higher costs.

    On the receiving side of the plant the feathers are pumped over a dewatering screen, which removes water from the feathers before they are transferred onto a feather press.

  • 08

    Feather presses

    The feather press further removes water still left in the product after the dewatering screen, to an acceptable percentage, before the product moves onto the holding bin for the next cooking cycle.

  • 09

    Feather holding bins (dragline)

    After the feather press the product moves onto a dragline (holding bin), ready to be loaded into the cooker for the next cooking cycle.

    This holding bin holds enough product for three cooking cycles.

  • 10

  • 12

    Design, manufacturing and installation of biological filter systems

    We design, manufacture and supply biological filters. Biological filters are placed after the condensers to eliminate vapour and odour into the atmosphere.

    Ideally, environmental impact and air pollution should be zero, and this system offers the best possible way to achieve this goal.
